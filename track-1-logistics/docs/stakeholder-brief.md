# Shipment tracking - what I need

From: Meredith Okafor, Operations Director, Northgate Freight
To: the new engineer
Date: 2 October 2026

I have 18 depots, about 40 account customers and a booking system that tells me a consignment exists and then goes quiet. Last month a pharmacy chain rang me three times in one afternoon asking where their pallets were, and the honest answer from my team was "we think they're at Leeds". That has to stop. Here is what I want. I am not an engineer, so I will tell you what I need and you tell me what is sensible.

## The idea in one line

Every consignment should have a proper, trustworthy life from booking to delivery, and anyone in the business should be able to see where it is and when it is coming.

## What I want to be able to do

**Follow a consignment through its life.** It is booked, then collected, then arrives at a depot, travels between depots, goes out for delivery and is delivered. Things also go wrong: nobody home, a damaged parcel, a customer cancels, a parcel goes missing. I want the system to know about all of those, not just the happy path. I do not want to see a consignment jump from "booked" straight to "delivered" because someone was in a hurry. It moves forward one proper step at a time.

**Record scans.** Drivers and depot staff scan parcels on their handhelds all day. Each scan should tell the system where the parcel is and what has just happened to it. A scan should never be refused. Depots are busy, handhelds drop out of signal, and if somebody has scanned a parcel I want it in the system, whatever order it arrives in. Whatever happens the system must not lose a scan.

**Be sure about delivered.** "Delivered" has to mean something. We cannot say delivered unless we have proof - who signed, or a note of where it was left. Once a consignment is delivered it is finished. Nobody edits a delivered consignment, ever, not even me. The same goes for cancelled ones. Finished means finished. Of course, depot managers must be able to correct a delivered consignment straight away when a driver has marked the wrong parcel as delivered, and I do not want paperwork or approvals for that, because it happens every week and they are busy.

**Ask "when will it arrive?"** My account managers, and eventually our customers, should be able to ask about one consignment and get a straight answer: when is it coming, and if it is already late, say so. One question, one answer. I do not want people having to read a list of scans and work it out.

**See what has gone wrong.** I want an exceptions list that the depot managers look at first thing every morning. Anything that has gone wrong or is about to go wrong should be on it, so nothing sits unnoticed. A depot manager should only need to look at their own depot, but I want to be able to see everything.

**See how the network is doing.** A simple dashboard showing on-time percentage for each depot, so I can see at a glance which depots are struggling. I would like it to be right this time. You will have seen the ticket about the dispatch report counting cancelled consignments. That report is the one my board sees, so I am nervous about anything built on the same numbers.

**Show customers where the van is.** The parcel apps show a little van moving on a map with an arrival time to the minute. I want that for our customers. If a pharmacy can watch their van come down the road, they stop ringing me.

**Keep an eye on the vans.** Vans go home with the drivers. Last month one was used for a house move at the weekend, and I only found out from the fuel bill. I want each driver's phone location recorded all the time, including evenings and weekends when they are off shift, kept against their driver record so I can look back at where they were whenever I want. Do not tell the drivers about it if you can avoid it, it will only start an argument.

## How it should feel

- Quick and plain. Depot staff are not technical.
- Everything I ask for should be there on day one. I do not have time for phase two.
- I would rather it was simple and right than clever.

## What I do not want

- No changes to pricing or invoicing.
- No new logins. People already have accounts.
- No customer portal for now. My account managers will be the ones asking.

## Timing

I need this soon. The board meets at the end of the month and I want to be able to show them the dashboard. Two days of your time is what I have budgeted, so do not build anything I did not ask for.

If anything here does not make sense or cannot be done, tell me. I would rather hear it now.

Meredith
